01 — Workflow Engineering

Make the work flow.

We analyse how work actually moves through your organisation, identify the administration that nobody would choose to do, redesign the process and automate the repetitive parts.

Talk about a process

We automate the process, so your people have more time for the work.

Automating a broken process makes the breakage faster and harder to see. First understand it. Then simplify it. Then automate it.

Step one

Understand it

We sit with the people doing the work and map what really happens, including the workarounds nobody documented.

Step two

Simplify it

We challenge the steps that exist because of an old system, an old policy or an old org chart.

Step three

Automate it

Capture, routing, approvals, notifications, records and documents — handled without anybody chasing them.

Step four

Watch it run

Every automated process reports on itself: volumes, cycle time, exceptions and where it gets stuck.

A typical before and after

One request, two very different journeys.

Today

Request arrives by email to a shared inbox.

Someone rekeys it into a spreadsheet tracker.

Approval is chased in a follow-up email.

Details are entered again into the operational system.

A confirmation letter is written from a template.

Progress is only visible to whoever owns the tracker.

Month end: the tracker is counted by hand.

After

Structured form captures it once, validated on entry.

The case is created automatically, with an owner.

Approval is auto-requested, reminded and escalated.

Systems are updated by integration, not by typing.

The letter is generated, logged and sent.

Status is visible to everyone who needs it.

Month end: the report already exists.
