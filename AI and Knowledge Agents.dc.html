03 — Frontline Knowledge & AI Agents

Give your people the organisation's knowledge when they need it.

Your organisation knows how to do the work. That knowledge is spread across policies, procedures, manuals, training material, SharePoint sites and experienced colleagues — which is why finding the right answer takes so long.

Talk about a knowledge assistant
The questions staff actually ask

"How do I process this type of application?"

"What do I do if a customer reports this issue?"

"Which approval do I need for this expenditure?"

"What's the procedure for escalating this case?"

Today those questions go to whoever is nearest, most senior or least busy. Data Cookie builds secure, controlled assistants that answer from your approved material — and then point the employee back to the authoritative source, so the policy remains the policy.

Augmentation, not replacement.

AI should remove cognitive and administrative burden while people stay responsible for the decisions that require judgement. An assistant that answers from approved policy and shows its source is useful. One that invents an answer is a liability.

How we keep it safe

Controlled, sourced and auditable.

Approved material only

The assistant answers from the documents you nominate, with a clear owner for each one.

Citations back to source

Every answer links to the policy, page or procedure it came from.

Permissions respected

People see what their role already entitles them to see, and nothing else.

Human in the loop

Drafting, summarising and guiding — with a person approving anything consequential.

Questions become insight

What staff repeatedly ask tells you which process or policy needs rewriting.
