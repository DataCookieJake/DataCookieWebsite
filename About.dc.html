About Data Cookie

Data Cookie is a digital transformation and workflow automation consultancy. We work with medium-sized organisations whose operational complexity has outpaced their systems — where capable people spend too much of the week on administration that a well-designed process would have absorbed.

We are not an AI consultancy. AI is one capable tool among several, and it is rarely the first thing a struggling process needs. What organisations usually need first is someone to understand the work, remove the steps that should not exist and connect the systems that should have been talking all along.

Technology should remove administrative burden, so people can spend more time doing the work that actually requires people.

That is the entire philosophy. Everything we build is judged against it.

Selected work
01 — Workflow Engineering

International programme

Replacing spreadsheet reporting with structured data capture

Partner organisations submitted operational returns through differently structured spreadsheets, leaving analysts to clean, reconcile and consolidate the data by hand before it could be used.

We redesigned the reporting process around a common data model and a purpose-built web form, so partners submit in a consistent structure with validation at the point of entry. The data flows straight into the analytical environment — removing consolidation work and making every submission analysis-ready the moment it arrives.

02 — Data & Decision Intelligence

Housing organisation

Cutting friction from the voids process

Disconnected housing, property and repairs systems meant staff tracked void properties and maintenance by hand, slowing the return of homes to use.

We replaced the spreadsheet processes with a governed data layer and live analytics, and connected the source systems: a property becoming void now triggers alerts and maintenance workflows, and repair completion updates its status — fewer handoffs, and real-time visibility of void times and bottlenecks.

03 — Knowledge & AI Agents

Frontline service organisation

Organisational knowledge at every employee's fingertips

High turnover and limited onboarding capacity left frontline employees navigating extensive policy and process documentation, while experienced staff answered the same routine questions repeatedly.

We consolidated approved policies and procedures into a frontline knowledge agent giving real-time, source-backed guidance on what to do next — easing the load on experienced colleagues, accelerating onboarding and putting guidance at the point of need.
